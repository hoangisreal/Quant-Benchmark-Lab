## Research conclusions

- Ollama usage exposes native cache/input/output counters and nanosecond durations in the
  terminal streaming response. Preserve these separately from observed TTFT/E2E latency.
  [Official usage reference](https://docs.ollama.com/api/usage).
- The selected llama.cpp stats count generation steps after the prefill-sampled first token.
  Source inspection supports `max(0, predicted_n - 1)` for this candidate; Ollama forwards
  its bundled server's counters/duration. This is revision-specific, not a general API rule.
  [Standalone stats](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/b92761a515ea31e852e7fbc1fad5f874b46f3718/tools/server/server-common.h#L433),
  [Ollama mapping](https://github.com/ollama/ollama/blob/b0c1ca4f7549d7acdfa52a7dcffc934bc63a43ce/llm/llama_server.go#L1824).
- Qwen3's official non-thinking template switch is supported; its recommended non-thinking
  sampling differs from this lab's shared greedy baseline. Keep the baseline for controlled
  comparisons and limit quality claims accordingly.
  [Pinned model card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-4B/blob/1cfa9a7208912126459214e8b04321603b3df60c/README.md).
- Qwen2.5-3B has the Qwen Research License and is not covered by the other candidates'
  Apache license. Keep its actual identity and comply with its research/evaluation terms.
  [Pinned license](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct/blob/aa8e72537993ba99e69dfaafa59ed015b17504d1/LICENSE).
- uv's `--frozen` uses an existing resolution without checking freshness. CI therefore
  checks lock presence and `uv lock --check` before frozen installation.
  [Official locking/syncing guide](https://docs.astral.sh/uv/concepts/projects/sync/).

## Remaining methodology limits

The six performance IDs have distinct texts but repeat one artificial word pattern across
three nominal length buckets. These labels are not verified token lengths; use recorded
native counts. The 60 quality tasks are balanced across exact/numeric/JSON categories but
are narrow project-authored exercises; the 20 exact-match items repeat five unique questions.
Greedy decoding, natural EOS, different tokenizers, sampled memory and model-cold OS caches
all constrain interpretation. See [limitations](../reference/limitations.md).

The current engine pair has a retained real CPU integration pass; GPU lifecycle, per-cell
monitor overhead, full GPU offload, near-limit attribution and official campaigns remain
unverified. The 4B GGUF artifacts are not yet resolved. No general speed or quality ranking
can be concluded from this audit or synthetic demonstrations.
